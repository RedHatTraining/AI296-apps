# RHEL AI Model Training Scenario: A Fictional Hotel Group

A fictional example for the _Training Large Language Models with Red{nbsp}Hat Enterprise Linux AI (AI0005L)_ lesson.
This lesson presents students with a scenario where a hotel group with three hotels must train their own LLM, aligned with their business needs
